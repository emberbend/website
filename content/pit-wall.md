---
title: Pit Wall
---

Pit Wall is a wall-mounted dashboard for my home. It shows upcoming events from my Google Calendar at a glance.

It's a personal project. I'm its only user, and it isn't offered to the public.

Pit Wall requests read-only access to Google Calendar. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) for how that data is stored and how to delete it, and the [Terms of Service](/terms/).
