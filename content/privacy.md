Ember Bend is run by Aaron Miler, an individual in Oregon, USA.

## This website

emberbend.com sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses.

## Google user data

[Pit Wall](/pit-wall/) requests read-only access to your Google Calendar to show upcoming events on a wall-mounted display. It stores your calendar data on a private server I operate. It does not share that data with anyone.

Pit Wall's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy#additional_requirements_for_specific_api_scopes), including the Limited Use requirements.

## What I don't do

I don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Deleting your data

Revoke access at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). To have stored data deleted, email [hello@emberbend.com](mailto:hello@emberbend.com).
